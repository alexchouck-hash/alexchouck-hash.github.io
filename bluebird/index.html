    How Bluebird forecasts

    Live, open data

    Global weather models (ECMWF, GFS, ICON and more), marine forecasts, USGS river gauges and satellite ocean data, fetched fresh in your browser.

    Built for the activity

    Each site turns raw weather into what matters for that day out: powder on a run, peak color, a runnable river level, a safe sea for a small boat.

    Checked against reality

    Where ground truth exists we score ourselves. Snow forecasts are verified daily against SNOTEL stations, and the blend learns from it.
