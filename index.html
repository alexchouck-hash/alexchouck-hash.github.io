Free tools

Public FDA data, no account, no charge. Built because the information is already public and assembling it is the tedious part.

  • PredicateScope

    Every 510(k) under a product code, with the review times computed from the public record.

  • Clearance Timeline Estimator

    How long 510(k) decisions actually took under a product code — as a distribution, from a dated snapshot.

  • Opportunity Analyzer

    Score a device idea on technical feasibility, market desirability and financial viability against a stated rubric.

  • Catheter Shaft Calculator

    Braid angle, coverage and wall stack for a catheter shaft, computed from published relations that are cited on screen.

  • Consensus Standards Lookup

    Search FDA's recognized consensus standards, see how far each one is recognized, and export a shortlist you selected.

  • Federal Funding Routes

    US federal funding routes — grants, loans and equity — with the page every figure was read from, screened against what you tell us.

Free, no account, and they run in your browser — what each one reads and what it will not do.

How we work

Fixed prices

Every line is quoted as a fixed price against a written scope before work starts. If the scope turns out to be bigger than we thought, we say so at quote time, not in an invoice.

Named human reviewers

AI drafts and accelerates; people decide and sign. Every AI-assisted deliverable ships with a review record naming who reviewed what, and what they corrected.

Selective intake

Every engagement starts with a scoping review. We take on a small number of concurrent programs so the engineers named on the review record are the ones doing the work, and we say so at the review when we’re not the right fit.
