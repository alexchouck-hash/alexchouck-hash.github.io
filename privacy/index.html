Who we are

This site is operated by 10Xand.com (“we”, “us”). We publish a postal address in the footer of every issue of our email brief, and will send it on request. Questions about this policy, or a request to delete your data, can be sent to {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • We do not set cookies, and we do not run advertising or tracking pixels on this site. Embedded videos load only when you press play.
  • Analytics are aggregate and cookieless. We cannot identify you from them.
  • If you give us your email address, we use it to send you what you asked for and our weekly brief, until you unsubscribe.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it except with the processors named below.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a cookieless analytics service, to count visits and understand which pages people read. Plausible does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and does not track visitors across sites or over time. It records aggregate data such as the page viewed, the referring source, approximate country, and device type, and it derives a daily, non-reversible identifier that cannot be used to recognise you tomorrow or elsewhere.

We also record custom events on this site — for example, that a page was scrolled to 75%, that a FAQ item was opened, or that a link to another service page was clicked. These events are aggregate counts. They carry the page and the element interacted with; they do not carry anything about you.

Email capture

When you submit a form on this site you give us an email address and, optionally, a name. We record which page the form was submitted from, so we know which material to send you.

Email is processed by Kit (ConvertKit), our email service provider. We use confirmed (double) opt-in: after you submit the form, Kit sends a confirmation email, and you are added to the list only if you click the link in it. You receive the material you requested and a weekly device-industry brief. Every message carries an unsubscribe link that works immediately and permanently.

We use this address to contact you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or trade it.

Booking a call

Scheduling is handled by Calendly. If you book a call, Calendly collects the information you enter on its scheduling page and processes it under its own privacy policy. We receive the booking details.

Videos

Some pages offer a tool walkthrough hosted on YouTube. Nothing is requested from YouTube or Google until you press play. Until then the page shows a still image stored on our own server. If you do press play, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com, and from that point YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback — it may set storage in your browser and will know that a video was watched. If you never press play, no request is made and nothing is set.

The free tools

The three free tools — PredicateScope, the Clearance Timeline Estimator and the Opportunity Analyzer — behave differently from the rest of the site, so they are worth separating out. None of them asks you to sign in, and none of them sends what you type to us.

PredicateScope queries FDA directly from your browser. Your search goes to api.fda.gov, which is an FDA service, and not to us. We see the aggregate page view and a named event recording that a search happened; the event carries the three-letter FDA product code at most, and never the text of a device or applicant search.

The Clearance Timeline Estimator makes no external request at all. It reads a dated snapshot file that shipped with the page.

The Opportunity Analyzer keeps your answers in your own browser so that reloading the page does not lose your progress. Those answers are never transmitted. If you submit the email form on it, the submission carries your email address and a tag — never your answers, and never your score.

These tools use session storage, which is not a cookie. PredicateScope caches FDA's responses so it does not re-ask for the same records; the Opportunity Analyzer holds your answers; all three remember that you dismissed a newsletter offer. Session storage is never sent to any server, is readable only by this site, and your browser clears it when you close the tab. We still set no cookies anywhere on this site.

The component navigator

The navigator on our component pages works in two ways, and they have different privacy consequences, so it is worth separating them.

Picking a device shape sends nothing anywhere. The matching runs entirely in your browser against data already on the page. No request is made, and we learn nothing beyond the aggregate page view.

Typing a free-text description sends that text to us so it can be turned into structured attributes. If you use that box, we store the description you typed, what we parsed it into, and whether we found a match. We do that for one reason worth stating plainly: a query we could not answer is a page we should have written, and the misses are how we decide what to build next.

We do not store your IP address alongside it. Rate limiting uses a hashed value that is not retained with the query. We do not ask for and do not want personal information in that box — it is for describing a device, and a description of a device is what we keep.

Query records are retained for {{NAVIGATOR_LOG_RETENTION}} and then deleted. If you would like a query of yours removed sooner, email {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}} — though bear in mind that with no identifier stored, you will need to tell us roughly what you typed and when.

Processors

We share data only with the services required to run this site:

  • Plausible Analytics — aggregate, cookieless site analytics.
  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email list management and delivery.
  • Calendly — call scheduling, only if you book.
  • YouTube (Google) — video playback, only if you press play on a walkthrough.
  • Anthropic — reads a free-text device description you type into the navigator, in order to structure it. Only if you use that box.
  • Our hosting provider — serves the pages and keeps standard server logs.

Each of these is bound by its own agreement with us and by its own privacy policy. Some are located in, or transfer data to, the United States.

Server logs

Our web server keeps standard access logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent — for security and troubleshooting. They are retained for {{LOG_RETENTION_DAYS}} days and then deleted.

Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on consent for marketing email (given by confirming your subscription) and on legitimate interest for aggregate analytics and server logs. You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, to object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable form. Where the CCPA or CPRA applies, you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of sale — noting that we do not sell personal data.

To exercise any of these, email {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Unsubscribing from the newsletter is faster, and the link is in every issue.

Retention

Email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and are removed from active lists immediately on unsubscribe. Booking records are retained for {{BOOKING_RETENTION}}. Aggregate analytics contain no personal data and are retained indefinitely.

Children

Changes
