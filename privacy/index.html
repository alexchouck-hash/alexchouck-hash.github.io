Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a cookieless analytics service, to count visits and understand which pages people read. Plausible does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and does not track visitors across sites or over time. It records aggregate data such as the page viewed, the referring source, approximate country, and device type, and it derives a daily, non-reversible identifier that cannot be used to recognise you tomorrow or elsewhere.

We also record custom events on this site — for example, that a page was scrolled to 75%, that a FAQ item was opened, or that a link to another service page was clicked. These events are aggregate counts. They carry the page and the element interacted with; they do not carry anything about you.

Email capture

When you submit a form on this site you give us an email address and, optionally, a name. We record which page the form was submitted from, so we know which material to send you.

Email is processed by Kit (ConvertKit), our email service provider. We use confirmed (double) opt-in: after you submit the form, Kit sends a confirmation email, and you are added to the list only if you click the link in it. You receive the material you requested and a weekly device-industry brief. Every message carries an unsubscribe link that works immediately and permanently.

We use this address to contact you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or trade it.

Booking a call

Scheduling is handled by Calendly. If you book a call, Calendly collects the information you enter on its scheduling page and processes it under its own privacy policy. We receive the booking details.

Videos

Some pages offer a tool walkthrough hosted on YouTube. Nothing is requested from YouTube or Google until you press play. Until then the page shows a still image stored on our own server. If you do press play, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com, and from that point YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback — it may set storage in your browser and will know that a video was watched. If you never press play, no request is made and nothing is set.

Processors

We share data only with the services required to run this site:

  • Plausible Analytics — aggregate, cookieless site analytics.
  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email list management and delivery.
  • Calendly — call scheduling, only if you book.
  • YouTube (Google) — video playback, only if you press play on a walkthrough.
  • Our hosting provider — serves the pages and keeps standard server logs.

Each of these is bound by its own agreement with us and by its own privacy policy. Some are located in, or transfer data to, the United States.
