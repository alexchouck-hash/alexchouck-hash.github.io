How this works

Usage baseline. Each player's stat line (attempts, carries, targets, yards, TDs) is a recency-weighted average of this season's games, each older game worth 75% of the next, blended with last season's per-game line counted as three games.

Game environment. Implied team points come from the spread and total (sportsbook consensus via nflverse, averaged with Kalshi's game-winner and total markets when available). The baseline is scaled by implied points over the team's recent scoring, and favorites tilt slightly toward rushing, underdogs toward passing.

Range. Floor and ceiling are roughly the 10th and 90th percentile, from each player's own week-to-week spread.

Not yet included: injuries, depth-chart changes, weather, and analyst consensus.
