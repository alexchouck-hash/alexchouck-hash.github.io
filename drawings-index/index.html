Drawing contact sheet

Every drawing at the sizes it actually ships at. Review here, not in a design tool — a tile that reads at 400px and turns to mud at 64px is exactly what this page is for.

Primitives, at usage size

Each specimen is rendered at its tier's real canvas, then shown at the sizes it appears on the site. Edge cases are included on purpose: a wall thinner than a stroke, and a coil whose pitch equals its wire diameter.

Category tiles

19 of 27 categories have a generated drawing. The remaining 8 render the placeholder mark and are the drawing backlog: Balloons, Coatings, Strain Reliefs, Core Wires, Stopcocks & Manifolds, Backer Cards & Inserts, Cartons & Shippers, Cable Ties & Strain Relief.

What to check

  • Legibility at 64px. If the lumen is ambiguous at tile size, the drawing has failed at its only job.
  • One highlight per drawing. Two highlights mean none.
  • Technical correctness on construction drawings. A wrong wall stack costs credibility with exactly the audience this site is for.
  • Stroke weight consistency across a tier, at every rendered size.
