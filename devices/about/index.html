How this catalog works.

What the data is, what it is not, and the rules we hold ourselves to. Written to be checked rather than trusted.

For healthcare professionals and institutional buyers. Product information is provided by or derived from manufacturer-public sources.

What this is right now

A concept build. Every manufacturer and product name here is a placeholder. There are no prices, nothing can be purchased, and no supplier relationship is implied by any listing. It exists to prove the navigation before real data goes in.

It currently holds 24 categories across 12 specialties, with 43 placeholder listings from 11 placeholder manufacturers. Three categories are fully exercised — surgical tables, OR ergonomics and hospital beds — because between them they cover capital equipment, accessories, and the classes where buying direct is plausible.

Where the data will come from

Every record carries two fields, and both are shown on the page:

SourceWhat it means
manufacturer-publicTaken from information the manufacturer publishes itself.
supplier-providedSubmitted to us by the supplier.
verifiedWe checked it against a primary source, and would show you which.

Alongside it, last_verified — the date somebody last confirmed the record. A specification with no date on it is a specification with no shelf life, and in capital equipment that matters: configurations change without the model name changing.

Neutrality

Inclusion and ranking are never for sale.

  • No paid placement, ever. Not a promoted tier, not a boosted result, not a "featured" slot that happens to correlate with revenue.
  • No "recommended" sort. Sorting is by name, manufacturer, or newest listed — orderings you can verify. A relevance ranking nobody can inspect is exactly where a neutrality promise quietly dies.
  • Relationships are labelled. Where a listed company has any relationship with our alliance, that is stated visibly on the profile and on every card it appears in. Disclosed because it exists — and it does not affect placement.
  • No comparative or clinical claims. Descriptions say what a thing is and what it does. This catalog will not tell you one device performs better than another; that is not a claim a catalog is entitled to make.

Prices

There are none here, and that is deliberate rather than unfinished. Capital equipment pricing is configuration-dependent and negotiated, so a number on a catalog page would be wrong the moment it was written. Listings show Quote, and the quote request goes to a human.

How listing works

Listing is free. A manufacturer or rep group can correct a record, add documents, or ask to be removed. We would rather hold a smaller catalog that is right than a bigger one that is stale.

Real names replace placeholders only after the naming posture is settled with our attorney. That is an open question — a neutral catalog referring to products by their actual names is ordinary and lawful in principle, but "in principle" is not the standard we ship on.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no cart, no checkout, no payment processing — none of that code exists in this build.
  • No tracking of individuals. Analytics are aggregate and cookieless, as everywhere else on this site.
  • No selling of buyer interest to manufacturers. A quote request goes where you send it.
