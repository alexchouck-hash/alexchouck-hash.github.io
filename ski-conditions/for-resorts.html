What you get

Every run, every hour

Surface conditions on each mapped run at its top, middle and bottom: powder, corduroy, corn, crust, ice. Driven by elevation, aspect, sun, wind loading, grooming and skier traffic.

Snowmaking windows

Hours of snowmaking temperatures (by wet-bulb) at the base, mid-mountain and summit for the next 10 nights, with prime hours flagged.

Grooming priorities

Tonight's list of runs most likely to end the day crusty, bumped or slushy and refreeze, weighted by traffic.

Lift wind holds

Gusts at each lift's top station against typical limits for chairs, gondolas, trams and surface lifts, for the next 3 days.

Storm ranges, not guesses

Low, likely and high totals from 4 global and up to 5 high-resolution models, powder-day chances, snow density ("champagne" to "cement"), snow line and rain-on-snow alerts.

A report that writes itself

A drafted morning snow report from the forecast, ready to edit and publish, plus a free widget for your website.

Accuracy you can check

Every day we save our forecast and score it against SNOTEL snow stations. The scores, by model and by days ahead, are published on each resort's forecast page. Our blend weights each model by how accurate it has actually been, at your resort once there's enough data. With a partner resort, we can verify against your own snow stake and weather station and tune the model to your mountain.

Data feed for your apps and screens

The same forecast, as JSON for every resort, is rebuilt every 6 hours. It covers daily summit, mid and base snowfall with ranges, powder chance, snow type, snow line, snowmaking hours, best runs, lift wind holds, every run's surface, and season-to-date against normal. Use it in your app, website, lift-line screens or an AI assistant.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alexchouck-hash/alexchouck-hash.github.io/data-feed/ski/forecast/vail.json (swap in any resort id; index.json lists them all).
