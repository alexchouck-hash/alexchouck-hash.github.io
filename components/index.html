How this is organised

Component categories are canonical and device-agnostic. Hypodermic tubing shows up under both reusable instruments and needles, but it has exactly one page — device pages reference it rather than copying it. 16 categories are currently shared this way.

Every drawing here is generated in-repo from the same numbers the category is about, so a cross-section cannot quietly disagree with a spec table. None of it is traced from anyone's catalog art. The rules are written down in DRAWING_STANDARD.md.

What is here today

  • 14 device types covering cardiovascular, endoscopy, surgical robotics, active implants, orthopedics, drug delivery, diagnostics, and surgical instrumentation.
  • 55 component categories with CAD-accurate parametric vector illustrations and plain engineering descriptions of what each does.
  • Recommended suppliers & ratings (2–3 per category) with direct outbound website links, technical specialties, locations, and ISO 13485 certifications.
  • Stock vs custom offering badges indicating fast-turn prototype availability, lead times, and MOQs.
  • Service Atlas — companion engineering services directory covering prototyping, 3D printing, CAD drafting, precision CNC machining, laser cutting, micro-welding, custom extrusion, coatings, testing, IEC 60601-1 safety, cleanroom CDMO, and sterilization.

What is coming
