What each one does

The grid is the default view. The table is the same information for anyone who would rather read than scan.

CategoryWhat it does on this deviceSpecs that matter first
PTFE Liners The inner lumen liner. Its outer surface is usually etched so the jacket bonds to it during reflow. ID, wall, etch
Reflow Tubing Polymer jacket segments — typically a durometer ladder along the shaft — reflowed over the reinforcement. durometer, OD, length
Braid Woven wire reinforcement laminated into the wall. Buys torque response and kink resistance; costs stiffness and wall. PIC count, pattern, wire
Coils Wound reinforcement. Trades braid’s torque transmission for hoop strength and flexibility. wire, pitch, flat/round
Hypotubes Thin-wall metal tubing for the proximal shaft, often laser-cut to grade stiffness toward the distal end. OD, ID, cut pattern
Marker Bands Dense metal bands swaged into the distal shaft so the tip is visible under fluoroscopy. material, OD, width
Soft Tips The atraumatic distal end, bonded to the shaft and usually the softest polymer on the device. durometer, length, taper
Balloons Blown or formed balloons bonded to the shaft; compliance class drives the whole design. diameter, length, compliance
Coatings Surface treatments — usually hydrophilic on the distal section — applied as a service, not a part. hydrophilic, length
Hubs & Luers The proximal termination and its fitting; luer taper geometry is specified by ISO 80369-7. ISO 80369-7, bore
Strain Reliefs The moulded transition at the hub that stops the shaft kinking where stiffness changes abruptly. length, durometer
Heat Shrink Process tubing — FEP or PET — used to compress the stack during reflow, then removed or left in place. FEP/PET, ratio
Core Wires Ground core wires that set the stiffness profile along the length of a wire or delivery system. material, taper, OD
