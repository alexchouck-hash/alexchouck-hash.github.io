Forecasts are estimates

Beach, sargassum, snow, weather, water, crowd and other forecasts come from models and third-party data (such as Open-Meteo, NOAA and the National Weather Service). They can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Beyond about 16 days, values are climate averages, not day-specific forecasts. Sargassum and crowd levels are modeled, not measured.
